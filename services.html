Frequently asked questions

How long will my website take?

Your website can take a maximum of two weeks to complete. We will agree on the schedule before work begins.

What should I provide?

Share your business details, the services you offer, your contact information, and any logo, text, or images you want included. We will discuss any content you still need.

Are domain and hosting costs included?

We will discuss domain registration and hosting options when preparing your quote, and clearly agree on which costs are included and which are paid separately.

Can you update my website after launch?

Tell me what updates you expect to need. We can discuss ongoing support or individual updates and agree on their scope and cost.

How do payments and revisions work?

We will agree on the payment schedule, the number of revision rounds, and what each round covers before work begins. Any extra work will be discussed and priced before it is carried out.
